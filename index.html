Questions

Frequently asked.

How long does company registration take?
Company registration with CIPC typically takes 3–5 working days. We handle the entire process — name reservation, registration documents and delivery of the final certificate.
What documents do I need for tax clearance?
You'll need your company registration certificate, proof of address, ID copy and recent financial statements. We guide you through every document and submit on your behalf to SARS.
Do you help with funding applications?
Yes. We assist with business plan preparation, financial projections and identifying suitable funding opportunities — from government grants to private investors.
Do you work with musicians and content creators?
Yes. Our creative division covers recording, music production, mixing and mastering, photography, branding, music videos, digital content, distribution and artist management. We also support artists with the business side — contracts, registration and formalisation.
Do you offer training for organisations and institutions?
Yes. Our training division delivers SMME training, entrepreneurship training, business-skills development, business-management training, mentorship, coaching and organisational workshops for both emerging enterprises and institutional clients.
What are your consultation fees?
Your first consultation is completely free. After understanding your needs, we provide a transparent quote with no hidden costs. Packages are tailored to your budget and scope.
